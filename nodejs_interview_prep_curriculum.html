Terminology precision

Use exact names: "V8 scavenge GC" not "garbage collection runs", "poll phase" not "event loop waits", "microtask checkpoint" not "the queue runs". Precise vocabulary signals seniority.

Structured performance answers

Always frame perf answers as: measure baseline → identify bottleneck → change one variable → re-measure. Never jump to solutions without establishing a measurement framework first.

Anchor to your projects

IoT license system → concurrency & throughput. CRM API improvements → performance profiling. HRMS → RBAC & security. Civic platform → data pipeline & reliability. Use these every time.

System design structure

Step 1: Clarify requirements + scale. Step 2: Capacity estimate. Step 3: High-level diagram. Step 4: Deep dive 2–3 components. Step 5: Identify tradeoffs + failure modes. Always do Step 1 before drawing anything.

Blank moment recovery

When stuck: "Let me think through this systematically…", "I'd approach this the same way I handled X in my CRM project, which is…", or "Can you tell me more about the scale requirements?" — all are professional pivots.
